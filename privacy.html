Privacy Policy — Jarvis

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Who uses this app

Jarvis is a private assistant used only by its owner's household, with their own Google accounts. It is not offered to the public.

What data it accesses

With the account owner's consent, Jarvis accesses that account's Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Docs, Sheets, and Contacts, solely to perform tasks the account owner requests, such as reading and sending email, managing calendar events, and working with documents.

How data is used and stored

Data is used only to carry out the owner's requests. Access tokens are stored locally on the owner's own computer. Data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Google API data

Jarvis's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
